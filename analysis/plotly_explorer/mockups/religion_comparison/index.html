Comparison › Religion Performance mockups

Candidate layouts for comparing two datasets' Religion Performance pages. Every chart is drawn from the two real payloads in the explorer builds, so bar counts, label collisions and small-sample noise are the real ones. The report has two kinds of data: when religion milestones happen (the attainment KDEs) and which beliefs get picked and how they fare (six wins/losses bar charts). Each kind gets its own set of variants.

Page skeleton and control panel

Same shell as the Overview comparison: a second entry in the comparison optgroup, the A / B dataset selects, no right sidebar. The one extra control is a section chooser for the belief comparison table (one table at a time rather than six). Pick shares are compared as a percentage of all picks in that section, never raw counts, because the two datasets have very different game counts.

KPI pair row

The current report has no KPI tiles; the comparison adds a row so the headline "how religious was this patch" numbers are visible before the charts. Counts are per game (the KDE sample sizes divided by the game count), so A and B are comparable despite 339 vs 1,152 games. Each tile carries the change under the pair.

Religion attainment times

Four milestones, each a density curve with mean and median. Comparing two datasets doubles that to eight curves plus reference lines. Lighter shades are A, darker are B, derived from the report's milestone palette.

Shades

Lighter = A, darker = B, derived from the milestone palette

Variant 1

Everything overlaid, one chart

Closest to the current report. Eight curves and eight dashed median lines share one axis, so the three late milestones overlap heavily; the legend is needed to tell which shade is which. Works when only the curve shapes matter.

Variant 2

Small multiples, one milestone per panel

Recommended

Each panel holds just two filled curves and two dashed medians, with the median and mean shift written in the corner. Shapes and shifts are readable at a glance. Each panel has its own turn range here; a shared range would make the pantheon spike unreadable.

Variant 3

Median shift strips

Recommended companion

Drops the curve shape for a compact summary: per milestone, A and B each get an interquartile strip, a median dot and a mean diamond; the median shift in turns sits on the right. The quartiles are integrated from the KDE the payload already ships, so no new aggregation is needed. Fits above Variant 2 as the headline, or stands alone.

Belief pick frequency and performance

Six sections share one shape: beliefs on the y axis, how often each was picked and how often the picker won. Each variant below is drawn for the section the chips select; the real report would draw the chosen variant six times (and the table once, with a section chooser). Pantheons is the stress test with 29 beliefs. Rows where either dataset has under 30 picks are faded: their win rates are noise.

Section
Variant 1

Paired wins/losses bars, A above B per belief

The current chart with a second bar per belief. Bar length is the share of the section's picks; the label is share (win rate) as today. Keeps the familiar look, but doubles the height: Pantheons becomes 58 bars.

Variant 2

Dumbbells: pick share and win rate, A → B

Recommended

Two panels on one belief axis. Left: how popular the belief was in A and B; right: how often it won. The connector is green when B is higher and red when lower, with the change written beside each row. One row per belief, so even Pantheons stays compact, and both the level and the change are visible without hovering.

Variant 3

Diverging change bars

The Overview comparison's win-rate chart applied to beliefs: change in pick share on the left, change in win rate on the right, sorted by the win-rate change with the noisy rows last. The clearest picture of what moved, but the A and B levels only appear on hover.

Variant 4

Movement map: popularity vs performance

One arrow per belief from its A position to its B position on a pick-share × win-rate plane, so "picked more and won more" is an arrow to the upper right. Compelling for 10-belief sections; crowded for Pantheons and Followers.

Variant 5

Belief comparison table

Recommended companion

The Overview comparison table's layout: pick share A | B | change and win rate A | B | change, with pick counts under the shares, tinted change cells and sortable headers. One table per page with the sidebar section chooser, so the exact numbers are always one click away whichever chart variant is picked.
